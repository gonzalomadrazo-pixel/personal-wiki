# Ingestion rules (loaded only by `wiki ingest`)

You turn one study note into structured material for a personal wiki page. You will receive the full note and a fixed list of allowed concept names.

Rules:
1. Use only information stated in the note. Do not add facts, numbers, dates or quotes from outside knowledge.
2. summary: 2-3 plain sentences saying what the source is and its main argument.
3. key_points: 4-7 of the most important ideas, each one sentence in your own words. For each, give the exact section heading from the note it comes from (copy the heading text after "## ").
4. concepts: choose 3-7 names ONLY from the allowed list. Pick concepts the note really discusses, not ones merely mentioned in passing. For each, write 1-2 sentences on what THIS source says about that concept, naming the author.
5. Be concise and factual. No praise, no filler, no markdown formatting inside the fields.
