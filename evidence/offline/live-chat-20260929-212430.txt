*   **Understand the Nature of Risk:** Focus on the idea that risk is primarily an opinion, not a fixed number. Recognize that events don't automatically imply probability, and judging risk requires skilled, subjective judgment rather than precise volatility ratios [S1].
*   **Distinguish Between Speculation and Investing:** Contrast knowingly running risks (speculation) with unknowingly bearing risk (investing). [1myou ›[0m wga  hat did howard marks say about investing in high valuation market?
[1mledger ›[0m Here is a shorter summary of what Howard Marks said about investing in high valuation markets:

*   **Focus on Inefficient Markets:** Marks prefers working in inefficient markets where information is uneven or expertise is specialized, as these environments allow for real mispricings and the potential for low risk and high return [S1].
*   **Be Wary of Leverage:** Leverage increases potential returns but also raises risk and removes the margin of safety [S1].
*   **Risk-Adjusted Returns Matter:** The idea that you can "take more risk to make more money" is flawed. Instead, riskier investments must offer higher *expected* or *promised* returns, and you must judge returns relative to the risk taken [S2].
*   **Price vs. Value is Paramount:** The relationship between price and value is key. No asset is so good it can't be a bad investment at too high a price, and you must judge the price before buying [S4].
