# Howard Marks

Co-founder of Oaktree Capital; his client memos are three of the sources.

## Sources in this wiki

- [[Marks 2001 Cycles Memo]] (2001): Howard Marks argues that while economic forecasts are unreliable, understanding the inevitable cycles and knowing one's current position within them is crucial for preparation.
- [[Marks 2003 Most Important Thing]] (2003): This study note outlines Howard Marks' core investment principles, emphasizing the importance of price and value, defensive investing, and avoiding reliance on macro forecasts.
- [[Marks 2006 Risk Memo]] (2006): This memo by Howard Marks focuses on redefining risk, arguing that volatility is not the same as risk and that true risk involves the likelihood of permanent loss or unacceptably low returns.
