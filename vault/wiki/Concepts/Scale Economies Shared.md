# Scale Economies Shared

*Topic group: Business Quality and Value.* Nomad's model: businesses that pass scale savings back to customers to grow a stronger moat.

## What the sources say

- **[[Nomad 2006 Annual Letter]]** (Nick Sleep): Sleep identifies this model, exemplified by Amazon and Costco, where passing efficiency and scale savings back to customers creates a virtuous cycle of more free cash flow.
