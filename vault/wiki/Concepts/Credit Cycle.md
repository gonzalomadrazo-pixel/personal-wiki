# Credit Cycle

*Topic group: Psychology and Cycles.* How lending standards loosen in good times and tighten in bad, amplifying booms and busts.

## What the sources say

- **[[Marks 2001 Cycles Memo]]** (Howard Marks): The credit cycle describes how changes in the economy cause swings in credit availability, where loosening standards during upswings and tightening during downswings amplify asset price movements.
