# Rule of Three

*Topic group: Process and Temperament.* Giverny's empirical rule about how often markets fall, stocks disappoint and a portfolio lags.

## What the sources say

- **[[Giverny 2002 Annual Letter]]** (Francois Rochon): François Rochon's Rule of Three outlines three market realities to accept in advance: a stock market fall exceeding 10% in one out of three years, one stock out of three that is disappointing, and the portfolio underperforming indexes in one out of three years. He describes this rule as an observed, not scientific, principle whose value is psychological, helping one act rationally during bad years and stock mistakes.
