# Owner Earnings

*Topic group: Business Quality and Value.* Judging investments by the underlying earnings of the businesses owned rather than by stock quotes.

## What the sources say

- **[[Buffett 1987 Letter]]** (Warren Buffett): Buffett judges holdings by their operating results rather than by daily or yearly stock quotes, focusing on the underlying earnings of the businesses.
- **[[Buffett 1996 Letter]]** (Warren Buffett): Buffett says a portfolio of companies whose aggregate earnings rise over the years will see its market value rise too, which is how Berkshire's look-through earnings and share price grew together.
- **[[Giverny 2002 Annual Letter]]** (Francois Rochon): Giverny measures itself by the growth in its companies' earnings and long-term prospects, not by short-term stock prices, following Buffett's look-through earnings idea.
