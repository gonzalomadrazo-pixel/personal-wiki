# Learning From Mistakes

*Topic group: Process and Temperament.* Reviewing past decisions honestly, including sales and missed purchases.

## What the sources say

- **[[Buffett 1996 Letter]]** (Warren Buffett): Buffett calls his 1989 USAir preferred-stock investment a mistake: he trusted the airline's long profit history and missed that deregulated competition had changed its economics, showing a track record can mislead.
- **[[Giverny 2002 Annual Letter]]** (Francois Rochon): Following J&J's habit of reviewing past acquisitions, Rochon looks back at his own decisions to learn from them.

## Related concepts

- [[Circle of Competence]]: many mistakes come from straying outside what you understand
- [[Rule of Three]]: some mistakes are expected and should be planned for
