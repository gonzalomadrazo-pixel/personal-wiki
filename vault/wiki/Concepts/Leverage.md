# Leverage

*Topic group: Risk.* Using borrowed money to magnify returns, and the fragility it creates.

## What the sources say

- **[[Marks 2003 Most Important Thing]]** (Howard Marks): Marks advises caution regarding leverage because it increases potential returns while simultaneously increasing risk and eliminating the margin of safety. He suggests that while exploiting unusually generous opportunities is acceptable, leverage is dangerous when trying to squeeze significant returns from small margins.

## Related concepts

- [[Credit Cycle]]: easy credit is what makes excessive leverage available
- [[Margin of Safety]]: leverage eats into the cushion that protects against loss
