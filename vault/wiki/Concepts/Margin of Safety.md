# Margin of Safety

*Topic group: Risk.* Paying a price low enough that the investment still works if things go wrong.

## What the sources say

- **[[Marks 2003 Most Important Thing]]** (Howard Marks): Marks cites Buffett's margin of safety as an ingredient in defensive investing, advising investors to pay prices low enough to ensure they can profit or at least not lose, even if circumstances change.

## Related concepts

- [[Price and Value]]: the margin is the gap between price paid and estimated value
- [[Leverage]]: borrowing shrinks or removes the margin of safety
