# Limits of Forecasting

*Topic group: Psychology and Cycles.* Why macro and market forecasts rarely add value, and how to invest without them.

## What the sources say

- **[[Marks 2001 Cycles Memo]]** (Howard Marks): Marks dismisses economic forecasts as adding little value, stating that markets already incorporate the consensus forecast.
- **[[Marks 2003 Most Important Thing]]** (Howard Marks): Marks advises investors to avoid relying on macro forecasts, as cycles are inevitable and extrapolating trends is dangerous, favoring a defensive approach.

## Related concepts

- [[Market Cycles]]: preparing for cycles replaces predicting them
- [[Margin of Safety]]: if you can't predict, build in protection instead
