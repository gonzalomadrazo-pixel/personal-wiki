# Capital Allocation

*Topic group: Business Quality and Value.* How managers deploy the cash a business generates, and why it matters to owners.

## What the sources say

- **[[Buffett 1987 Letter]]** (Warren Buffett): Buffett notes that many CEOs rise through marketing, production or administration and are not skilled at capital allocation, yet a CEO whose company retains 10% of net worth a year will have deployed more than 60% of its capital after ten years.

## Related concepts

- [[Owner Earnings]]: good allocation turns retained earnings into higher future earnings
- [[Aligned Incentives]]: managers allocate better when their interests match owners'
