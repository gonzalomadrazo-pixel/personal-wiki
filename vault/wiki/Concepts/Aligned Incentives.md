# Aligned Incentives

*Topic group: Process and Temperament.* Structuring fees, ownership and pay so managers and investors win or lose together.

## What the sources say

- **[[Munger 2016 Daily Journal Meeting]]** (Charlie Munger): Charlie Munger argues that large amounts of easy money lead to regrettable behavior, which he termed 'Munger's rule,' citing examples like venture rounds quietly subordinating earlier investors.
- **[[Nomad 2006 Annual Letter]]** (Nick Sleep): The firm structured its fees so that the management fee falls as the fund grows, and the performance fee structure ensures partners share risk by refunding them in years below the hurdle.
