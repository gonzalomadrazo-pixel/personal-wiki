# Mr Market

*Topic group: Psychology and Cycles.* Graham's allegory of an emotional market partner whose prices serve you but should not guide you.

## What the sources say

- **[[Buffett 1987 Letter]]** (Warren Buffett): Buffett retells Graham's allegory of Mr. Market, an emotional partner whose quotes swing between euphoria and depression; the investor is free to ignore him or take advantage of him, but must never fall under his influence.
- **[[Buffett 1993 Letter]]** (Warren Buffett): Buffett suggests that investors should welcome wild price swings because they mean solid businesses can sometimes sell at irrationally low prices, and an investor should ignore or exploit the market rather than be harmed by it.

## Related concepts

- [[Market Cycles]]: the pendulum of market psychology is Mr. Market's mood swings over time
- [[Contrarian Investing]]: exploiting Mr. Market means buying when he is depressed
