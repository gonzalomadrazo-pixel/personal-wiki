# Contrarian Investing

*Topic group: Psychology and Cycles.* Buying what others are avoiding, because only unpopular assets can be truly cheap.

## What the sources say

- **[[Marks 2001 Cycles Memo]]** (Howard Marks): Marks copes with cycles by leaning against the wind: investing in cyclical companies when the economy is depressed and observers are gloomy, and preferring arenas where others are not throwing money.
- **[[Marks 2003 Most Important Thing]]** (Howard Marks): Contrarian behavior is considered the most reliable approach because it suggests that only unpopular assets can be genuinely cheap.
