# Mental Models

*Topic group: Process and Temperament.* Thinking with many models from different disciplines instead of one formula.

## What the sources say

- **[[Munger 2016 Daily Journal Meeting]]** (Charlie Munger): Munger advocates for using multiple mental models to think about many things at once, stating that investing is never easy and that specialization alone can lead to mistakes elsewhere.
- **[[Giverny 2002 Annual Letter]]** (Francois Rochon): Echoing Munger's advice to use many mental models, Rochon compares investing to gardening: know your soil and climate, tend the garden continuously, and don't pull the flowers to water the weeds.
