# Risk vs Volatility

*Topic group: Risk.* Whether investment risk means price volatility (beta) or the chance of permanent loss.

## What the sources say

- **[[Buffett 1993 Letter]]** (Warren Buffett): Buffett argues that defining risk as volatility (beta) is wrong because it ignores the underlying business fundamentals, suggesting it is better to be approximately right than precisely wrong.
- **[[Marks 2003 Most Important Thing]]** (Howard Marks): Marks frames risk control as avoiding losers and bad years ('invest scared'), aiming over time for above-market returns with below-market volatility.
- **[[Marks 2006 Risk Memo]]** (Howard Marks): Marks argues that volatility is not risk because it is a measure chosen for convenience, and it does not accurately reflect how risk changes based on the current price level.

## Related concepts

- [[Mr Market]]: volatile quotes are Mr. Market's moods, which an investor can exploit rather than fear
- [[Margin of Safety]]: buying well below value is the practical defence against permanent loss
- [[Diversification vs Concentration]]: whether concentration is risky depends on how risk is defined
