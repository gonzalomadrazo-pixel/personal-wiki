# Patience and Long Holding Periods

*Topic group: Process and Temperament.* Why waiting, and holding good businesses for years, is a source of return.

## What the sources say

- **[[Buffett 1996 Letter]]** (Warren Buffett): Buffett advises investors to stick to a rule: if one would not own a stock for ten years, they should not own it for ten minutes.
- **[[Munger 2016 Daily Journal Meeting]]** (Charlie Munger): Munger describes making money by waiting, emphasizing that patience should be followed by aggressive action when a chance arises, as successful long-term outcomes often result from a few significant transactions over time.
- **[[Giverny 2002 Annual Letter]]** (Francois Rochon): Great companies, like trees, take decades to grow, and time and patience matter most when investing.
- **[[Nomad 2006 Annual Letter]]** (Nick Sleep): Sleep argues that holding good businesses for many years can raise and de-risk returns because business outcomes can be more predictable over several years than short-term results.

## Related concepts

- [[Rule of Three]]: accepting bad years in advance makes patience easier
- [[Opportunity Cost]]: patience means waiting for opportunities that beat the alternatives
