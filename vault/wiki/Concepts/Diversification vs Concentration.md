# Diversification vs Concentration

*Topic group: Risk.* How many positions to hold, and when owning few or many makes sense.

## What the sources say

- **[[Buffett 1993 Letter]]** (Warren Buffett): Buffett and Munger prefer concentration because it can reduce risk if it compels the investor to analyze a business thoroughly, rather than making many small, uncertain decisions.
- **[[Marks 2006 Risk Memo]]** (Howard Marks): Marks says diversification trades return for safety because knowledge of the future is imperfect; concentration is risky for investors who can't see the future clearly, but not for those who can.
- **[[Nomad 2006 Annual Letter]]** (Nick Sleep): Sleep lists over-diversification among the capital-destroying mistakes of index-relative investing; Nomad's top five holdings were about half of the portfolio.
