# Market Cycles

*Topic group: Psychology and Cycles.* The recurring, self-correcting swings of economies, companies and investor psychology.

## What the sources say

- **[[Marks 2001 Cycles Memo]]** (Howard Marks): Marks believes cycles are inevitable and that investors should focus on knowing where they are in a cycle rather than trying to predict its timing.
- **[[Marks 2003 Most Important Thing]]** (Howard Marks): Howard Marks argues that market cycles are inevitable because prices often move more than underlying fundamentals, making the extrapolation of trends a dangerous practice for investors.
- **[[Giverny 2002 Annual Letter]]** (Francois Rochon): François Rochon notes that the 2000-2002 market decline was mainly caused by shrinking P/E ratios, particularly among the largest companies.

## Related concepts

- [[Credit Cycle]]: credit availability is one of the strongest drivers of the wider cycle
- [[Limits of Forecasting]]: cycles can't be timed, but you can know roughly where you are
